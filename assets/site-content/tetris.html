DVK-2M Tetris

I recreated the original Tetris from Alexey Pajitnov's Soviet DVK-2M computer. I wrote the game logic myself and modeled the interface on the original version.

I mostly wanted something fun to play on the gymnase computers. The site eventually got blocked there.

Controls

  • 1 -- show next piece
  • 5 or Space -- drop
  • 7 or Left -- move left
  • 8 or Up -- rotate
  • 9 or Right -- move right
