Overview

Decks is the flashcard app I made after [a href="https://www.studysmarter.de/"]StudySmarter[/a] lost a large chunk of my study material. It supports LaTeX, images, and two ways to review cards. You bring your own Firebase database rather than storing everything in an account on my server.

It runs in a browser. You can move sets between installations using .deck files (JSON underneath), or share an import link.

Studying

  • Flashcard mode, with Hard / OK / Easy ratings
  • Box-based repetition: review cards in batches, repeating the difficult ones until they're learned. The name in the app says "spaced repetition," but it isn't a date-based SRS scheduler.
  • LaTeX through MathJax, including mathematical notation in the cards
  • Images uploaded to the user's Firebase Storage
  • Import and export, dark and light themes, keyboard shortcuts

Why I wrote it

I used to make flashcards at a fairly ridiculous speed. One week before my Euler Calculus I exam, StudySmarter lost a large part of them. I wasn't going to rebuild that collection inside the same app.

I wrote the first version of Decks in about a week and used it for the second part of the exam. The separate database and exportable files were deliberate: I wanted to be able to take my notes with me.
