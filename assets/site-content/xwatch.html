XWatch

2019. I strapped an M5Stack to my wrist and made it behave more like a little computer than a development board.

I recently found the old XWatch.ino source: over a thousand lines of C++, plus the graphics assets. It has a watch face, clock, alarm, sleep timer and a music player that reads MP3 files from SD storage. There's navigation, cover art and playback control.

I remember having it done around grade 7 but the file metadata is saying 2019, so I must have been 11/12 years old at the time, so 8th grade, so this project must have lasted a long time.

Details

The music player's four-bar "spectrum analyzer" was random. It looked convincing enough on a watch. The playback itself used an actual decoder and I²S audio output. I wrote the graphics around it. A 12 year old myself wouldn't have written an mp3 player from scratch.

Most of the work here was firmware and interface design. I have recently recovered the source code and published it [a href="https://github.com/adrianvla/xwatch-archive"]here[/a].
