Abstract

For my 2024–2025 Travail de maturité, I designed a 64-bit RISC processor in Verilog and ran Tetris on an Artix-7 FPGA. The game has video output and sound. I also wrote the assembler and the hardware around the processor.

The CPU runs at 40 MHz and has sixteen 64-bit registers. Its peripherals include eight GPIO lines and two 8-bit PWM channels for audio. The technical report contains the instruction-set design, implementation, tests and performance measurements. The measured power figure was 0.311 W for the evaluated design.

This was the second processor architecture I built. The [direct_a href="/project?2023-Processor"]first one[/direct_a], from 2023, had a much more complicated history with interrupts and a graphical emulator. I started over for the diploma.
