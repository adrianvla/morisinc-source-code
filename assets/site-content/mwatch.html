MWatch

Grades 8–9. I wanted a GSM watch I could actually wear. I used Seeed's Xadow/RePhone MT2502 electronics, then spent my time on the much less glamorous problem of fitting everything inside a watch case.

The build

I made the enclosure and arranged the hardware in a folded stack. The stock display-to-module connection didn't fit the layout, so I designed a replacement interconnect PCB and used flexible cables to join the parts. It was my first substantial PCB design. The physical watch was assembled and worked.

Body designed in TinkerCAD, PCB routed in KiCAD, rendered in Blender.

Old website

The original product website survives, along with renders of proposed Dark, Lime and Sky versions. I treated it like a product launch at the time, although the variants remained concepts. The actual prototype is still in a drawer.
