Datapacks

Later came Speedrunner vs Hunter, Death Swap, Herobrine, Survivalist vs Hitmen, acid rain, a cursed language pack and other challenges. They were released on [a href="https://www.planetminecraft.com/member/____keke____/submissions"]Planet Minecraft[/a] around 2020–2021. I also made regular YouTube videos around these releases while attending Cours Euler.

Creepler Network

In ninth grade I built a full server network, with BungeeCord, Paper/Spigot, Bedwars, custom UHC and a Modern Warfare-inspired mode. I integrated Geyser for Bedrock players and Dynmap for the web.

I wrote and administered the system myself. The server was public for about a month; the actual regular players were mainly two friends. I eventually stopped running it, but learned a ridiculous amount about servers and game logic along the way.

The website

I had a separate site at [a href="https://keke.morisinc.net"]keke.morisinc.net[/a] for sharing creations, along with a simple content system. Weekly recording, editing and publishing eventually (really quickly) became exhausting. I moved on to other projects.
