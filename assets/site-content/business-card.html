An opensource business card

A business card that's a PCB instead of cardboard seemed like a reasonable and fun idea, so I made one. The result is roughly credit-card-sized and runs on an nRF52, with NFC, BLE, an accelerometer and LEDs. The active build took about a week.

[img src="/assets/img/bc-photo.png"][/img]

The PCB

I began in Sketch, did schematics, board layout and routing in Altium, and wrote the firmware myself. A tap, flick or NFC interaction wakes the card from low power mode. The NFC tag carries a vCard; the board can advertise battery voltage over BLE and measure it with the nRF52's ADC. The accelerometer communicates over I2C with the nRF52.

[img src="/assets/img/bc-schem.png"][/img]

Presenter mode

The card also presents itself as a Bluetooth HID keyboard. Hold it face up and flick it left or right to advance presentation slides, with LED feedback for each gesture. It worked, and I made physical copies to hand to people.

If you meet me IRL, there's a non-zero probability that I'll give you one of those.
