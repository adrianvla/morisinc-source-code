About

I've been programming, building things, and experimenting with whatever catches my interest since childhood. Sometimes it starts with something I want to make, sometimes with a question I can't quite leave alone. One thing tends to lead to another, and I've often ended up exploring subjects I hadn't originally intended to.

I enjoy understanding how things work, but I'm equally interested in what that understanding might make possible. I like exploring unconventional approaches, connecting ideas from different areas, and trying things that haven't been done before. Sometimes the aim is simply to discover something new; other times, it's to develop something that could eventually help solve a much larger problem.

A lot of what I do begins with curiosity, but I don't want it to end there. I'd like my work to contribute something beyond my own understanding, whether that's new knowledge, useful technology, or something that makes a meaningful difference to other people.

My more formal mathematical education came through [a href="https://www.epfl.ch/education/education-and-science-outreach/fr/cours-euler/"]Cours Euler[/a], a five-year mathematics program at EPFL that I successfully completed in 2025. I also attended [a href="https://www.epfl.ch/education/education-and-science-outreach/fr/gymnasiens/cours-turing/"]Cours Turing[/a] for two years. Beyond the mathematics and computer science themselves, I particularly valued the opportunity to encounter different ways of approaching problems, and to meet people who saw things I wouldn't have thought of myself.

Art has been a major part of my life since childhood. I've studied classical piano, spent two years studying organ, and attended a [a href="https://hem.hesge.ch/formation/cursus-precollege"]pre-college[/a] music programme for one year. My creative interests extend far beyond music, taking me through composition, production, typography, web and product design, and many other forms of expression. I've never found the distinction between artistic and technical work particularly meaningful; creativity, experimentation, and precision have a place in both, and often come together in the same project. Languages and writing also occupy a lot of my attention; studying Japanese eventually led me to develop [direct_a href="/project?mLearn"]mLearn[/direct_a]. But even this list feels rather arbitrary. I can become interested in almost anything, and I'd rather keep discovering new subjects than decide in advance which ones are supposed to be mine.

This website brings together some of the things I've worked on and thought about over the years.
