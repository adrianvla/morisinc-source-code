Smart Glasses

Starting around age eight. I wanted an augmented display, and I kept drawing new ways to pack the electronics and optics into glasses. I remember something like fifteen form-factor revisions. Most were on paper or at the physical-prototype stage.

The difficult parts were the optics, positioning the image, and fitting everything into something wearable. I experimented with prism ideas and later explored Raspberry Pi Zero-based layouts. The display system never reached the point of working AR glasses.

Some old `PiPrism` files and models survived. I can't remember which optical assemblies I made myself and which I was testing from elsewhere, so I'm leaving their authorship unresolved.
