quick!

A small scheduling application using PERT to arrange tasks and dependencies. I wanted to see the schedule at a finer granularity than a normal to-do list gives you, down to minutes or seconds. Instead of being a life organizer, this became an anxiety machine.

The project is [a href="https://github.com/adrianvla/quick"]open source[/a]. The old hosted version was at [a href="https://quick.morisinc.net/"]quick.morisinc.net[/a]; its database setup follows the same user-managed approach documented for [a href="https://decks.morisinc.net/help"]Decks[/a].
